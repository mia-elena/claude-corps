Design Notes

  • Google Form stays as the intake surface — no change for volunteers signing up; Apps Script bridges it to Airtable automatically, replacing Priya's manual entry step.
  • Airtable remains the single source of truth for Name, Phone, Route, Shift, and Status — not the Sheet. The Sheet becomes a transient staging layer only.
  • Claude's role is generation + routing, not storage. It personalizes confirmation and reminder message text (e.g., includes correct shift time, location, cancellation instruction) and decides when to alert Slack. MCP servers handle all I/O.
  • No-show tracking becomes automatic: Airtable records who confirmed, who cancelled via SMS, and who appeared (future: Priya marks attendance). Monday report computes the no-show rate formally for the first time.
  • Weather cancellations (currently decided by Marcus by 6 AM): Claude can send a bulk cancellation SMS to the day's roster the moment Marcus posts to #warehouse-ops — no additional manual step. This replaces the current group-text and the wishlist cancellation banner.
  • Age constraint enforcement: mobile pantry shifts (18+) are flagged in the signup form itself — see updated index.html.
